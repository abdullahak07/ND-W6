MODULE 2 / 6

THREATS & INDICATORS

▸ BRIEFING
  • Modern attacks are multi-vectored and multi-staged: infiltrate → spread → exfiltrate
  • IoCs are the data fundamentals of an attack — the fingerprints it leaves behind
  • Three families: Network (IPs, URLs, domains), Host (hashes, DLLs, registry keys), Email (senders, attachments, links)

MODULE 3 / 6

SOURCING THREAT DATA

▸ BRIEFING
  • Internal logs, external feeds, and unstructured sources (forums, dark web) all feed CTI
  • Reliability varies wildly — vendor sites and vulnerability DBs score highest on trust
  • Blogs and misc. sources score lowest — no single source type excels everywhere

MODULE 4 / 6

SHARING & STANDARDS

▸ BRIEFING
  • Disjoint, siloed efforts produce fragmented threat analysis — no one sees the full picture alone
  • Many overlapping formats exist: STIX/TAXII, IODEF/RID, CybOX, OpenIOC
  • STIX is the de facto standard — modular, JSON-based, and extensible

THREAT INTEL PLATFORMS

▸ BRIEFING
  • TIPs collect, normalise, enrich, correlate and disseminate threat data automatically
  • MISP is the leading open-source platform; OpenCTI, CRITs and CIF round out the field
  • Today's gap: platforms collect far more than they analyse — trust is often an afterthought
